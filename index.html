Share the ride. Find your team.

Student cab pooling, matched by route and skills.

A campus app that pools students going the same way, then ranks them by skills so a cab ride to the airport or station becomes a high-value networking session.

The Idea

Post or request a ride, get matched by route and departure time in seconds. No more noisy, unorganized WhatsApp groups.

How It Solves It

One place replaces the chaos: verified campus students (@bennett.edu.in), live group chat, and instant cost splitting.

Why It Is Better

Existing apps only pool empty car seats. PoolMates pools people, ranking co-riders by complementary skills & interests.
